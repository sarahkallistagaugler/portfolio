More About Me

I'm Sarah, a graphic designer with over a decade of experience that began in 2014 when I launched my own freelance graphic design business while in high school.

I started my professional career on an in-house marketing team, advancing from student worker to leading an entire team of graphic designers. I was responsible for ensuring brand consistency across all materials and guiding my team to produce cohesive, high-quality work.

For the past four years at a small design agency, I progressed from graphic designer to Technical Design Lead - a role I proposed and helped define after recognizing the need for deeper technical specialization. I now lead our most technical projects (responsive HTML emails, interactive landing pages, complex document layouts), push forward advancements in our interactive and web-based design capabilities, and research AI tools to strengthen team workflows and creative output.

I'm detail-oriented to a fault - a trait that drives precision but has also taught me, through years of team collaboration and stakeholder feedback, how to balance rigor with flexibility. What motivates me most is combining strong visual craft with technical execution to deliver engaging, functional experiences.

My interest in the technical side of design accelerated dramatically with the rise of AI-assisted coding. Having learned basic HTML & CSS years earlier, I suddenly found I could prototype, build, and iterate on almost anything with AI support - no longer limited to "just" design. I see this as a major turning point for graphic design as a discipline, and I'm genuinely excited to keep leveraging these tools to push boundaries and create more ambitious, interactive work.

I'm eager to bring that blend of experience, leadership, and forward-looking technical curiosity to the next project.
